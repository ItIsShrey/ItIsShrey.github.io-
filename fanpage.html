Biography

From Akron to
Greatness

LeBron Raymone James Sr. was born on December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio. Raised in challenging circumstances, his extraordinary basketball talent was evident from an early age — earning him the nickname "The Chosen One" from Sports Illustrated before he even played a single NBA game.

Selected first overall by the Cleveland Cavaliers in the 2003 NBA Draft, LeBron immediately transformed the franchise and NBA's global profile. His career has spanned four teams — Cleveland, Miami, Cleveland again, and the Los Angeles Lakers — winning a 2020 NBA title with the Lakers in the pandemic bubble.

On February 7, 2023, LeBron surpassed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar to become the NBA's All-Time Scoring Leader with 38,388+ career points. He remains the only player in history to accumulate 40,000+ points, 10,000+ rebounds, 10,000+ assists, and 2,000+ steals — a statistical titan across two full decades.

Beyond Basketball

A Legacy Off the Court

LeBron's impact extends far beyond basketball records. Through the LeBron James Family Foundation, he has committed over $100 million to support at-risk youth in his hometown of Akron, Ohio.

The I PROMISE School, opened in 2018 in partnership with Akron Public Schools, provides wraparound support for at-risk students — including free tuition, meals, bikes and helmets, and a guaranteed scholarship to the University of Akron for all graduates.

LeBron is also a vocal advocate for social justice, civil rights, and voter empowerment. In 2020 he co-founded More Than a Vote, a coalition of Black athletes and artists fighting voter suppression and protecting the Black vote in America.
