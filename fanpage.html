Biography & Legacy

Born on December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio, LeBron James entered the NBA directly from high school as the #1 overall draft pick in 2003 by the Cleveland Cavaliers.

Over his legendary career with Cleveland, Miami, and the Los Angeles Lakers, he has won 4 NBA championships and broke Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's all-time scoring record in 2023.

Off the Court: In 2018, he founded the I PROMISE School in Akron, providing free education, meals, and guaranteed college scholarships for at-risk students.
